Our Story

125+ Years Bold

From a small pharmacy in North Carolina to a global icon — this is the Pepsi story.

1893

Born Bold

Caleb Bradham creates "Brad's Drink" in his pharmacy in New Bern, North Carolina.

1898

Pepsi-Cola

Bradham renames the drink Pepsi-Cola, inspired by the digestive enzyme pepsin and kola nuts.

1965

PepsiCo Founded

Pepsi-Cola merges with Frito-Lay to form PepsiCo, Inc., one of the world's largest food and beverage companies.

1984

The Choice of a New Generation

Michael Jackson joins Pepsi in a landmark partnership, igniting the "new generation" campaign era.

2023

Bold New Look

Pepsi unveils a refreshed logo and visual identity for the first time in 14 years — bolder, more modern, unmistakably Pepsi.
